# Efremidis_Addison_A2_VinylDJ

- Vinyl DJ 🎵

A personal music recommendation app that acts as my own virtual DJ by selecting a vinyl record from your collection based on mood and preferred genre.

- Introduction - 

Choosing what music to listen to can sometimes be harder than actually listening to it. As a passionate vinyl collector and music lover, I often found myself spending more time deciding on an album than enjoying one.

Vinyl DJ was created to solve that problem. By selecting a genre and mood from simple dropdown menus, the app recommends a record from my personal collection, helping me discover albums that match how I'm feeling while making the listening experience more spontaneous and enjoyable.

- How to Run - 

Download or clone the project files.
Open the project folder.
Double-click the index.html file.
From the menu bar on your computer click 'Run'
No additional software or installation is required.

- How It Works - 

Choose a genre.
Choose your current mood.
Click the recommendation button.
The app selects a record from the collection that matches your criteria.
Put the record on and enjoy the music!

- Why I Built This - 

This project combines two of my biggest passions: music and vinyl records.

As someone who is naturally indecisive when it comes to choosing what to listen to, I wanted to create a fun tool that removes the stress of decision-making while encouraging me to revisit records throughout my collection.

Also included in this repository are all the archived versions of my app starting from the beginning, to see the progress of where I started and what eventually becomes the completed version of my app.
